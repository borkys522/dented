THE CONDITION REPORT

CAN YOU GET BACK OUT

Before anything else: we simulate a real holder sending tokens into the pool, straight on the chain. If the contract refuses it, money goes in and does not come out, and nothing else on the page matters.

IS THE LIQUIDITY NAILED DOWN

Whether the launch position sits in the launchpad’s locker — and whether that locker even has a function to release it again. A lock nobody can open is the only kind worth counting.

WHO HOLDS IT

How much sits in the top ten wallets, how much the single largest holds, whether they are new addresses made for this launch — and whether any of them are sending into the pool right now.

WHAT CAN STILL BE DONE TO YOU

Whether the source is published, whether it can mint, blacklist, change fees or pause; whether anyone still owns it; whether the code can be swapped through a proxy; and whether the deployer has already put out a dozen of these.

WHAT IS BEHIND THE PRICE

The chart, the high it fell from, liquidity across every pool, how much of the market cap that liquidity actually backs, and trades in the last hour against its own average. A chart with nothing behind it is a picture.

THE LABEL

Site, X, Telegram — opened straight from the report. Whether somebody paid to put them there, whether the name leans on a real one, and how many other tokens on this chain already use the same ticker.

Why a dented can

"They shopped at Shoppers Food Warehouse rather than Safeway, because they have the dented cans."

Vlad Tenev, who founded Robinhood, on his childhood · "The Philosophies of Vlad Tenev", narrativ, 18 March 2026

A family newly arrived from Sofia, buying the damaged tins because damaged was cheaper. Two decades later the same person built the brokerage that let anyone buy a share of anything, and then put the shares on a chain that never closes.

On that chain, 850,000 tokens launched in 92 days and almost all of them are dented past use. Telling the discounted from the spoiled is the entire job of this page.
